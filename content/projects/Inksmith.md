###### Inksmith is a visual novel puzzle game made in RenPy.  
Around 30-45 mins of playtime.

>This project was made during MelonJam 2024
>The theme was "FLOW"

##### [See the itch page here](https://arielycliu.itch.io/inksmith)


## How to Play
You play as an "Inksmith", a god of words. You have to travel and jump between different timelines and characters to get certain key phrases. You choose which key phrases to give the characters to help them resolve whatever writing dilemmas they are stuck on. After solving all four timelines, you win the game.
